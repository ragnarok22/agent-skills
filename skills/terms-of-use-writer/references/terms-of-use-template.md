# Terms Of Use Template

Use this as a drafting structure for generated Terms of Use. Adapt sections to the product, jurisdiction, business model, and user instructions. Do not keep placeholder text in the final document unless the fact is genuinely missing.

## Default Document Structure

# Terms of Use

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

These Terms of Use govern access to and use of [Product Name], operated by [Company Legal Name] ("[Company Short Name]", "we", "us", or "our"). By accessing or using [Product Name], you agree to these Terms.

## 1. Acceptance of These Terms

Explain that use of the service means acceptance. State that users must not use the service if they do not agree.

## 2. About The Service

Describe the product in concrete terms based on the codebase and docs. Avoid marketing exaggeration.

Include:

- What users can do.
- Any platform/API/SaaS-specific capabilities.
- Any important limitations.

## 3. Eligibility And Accounts

Cover account creation, accurate information, credentials, responsibility for account activity, minimum age if known, and organization/team authority if relevant.

## 4. Plans, Payments, And Billing

Use this section when the product has paid access, subscriptions, usage billing, invoices, trials, credits, or checkout.

Address only relevant items:

- Prices and plan terms.
- Subscription renewal.
- Trial conversion.
- Payment authorization.
- Taxes.
- Failed payments.
- Usage-based or metered fees.
- Billing provider or third-party processor.
- Plan changes.

Avoid promising fixed prices forever unless explicitly required.

## 5. Cancellations And Refunds

State how users cancel and what happens after cancellation.

Address:

- Whether access continues until the end of the billing period.
- Whether refunds are offered.
- Whether partial refunds, credits, or exceptions apply.
- Any consumer-law caveat if needed: "except where required by law".

Use placeholders if the policy is unknown.

## 6. Acceptable Use

Define prohibited conduct. Tailor this list to the product.

Common prohibitions:

- Illegal, harmful, abusive, deceptive, or infringing activity.
- Interference with service operation or security.
- Unauthorized access, scraping, crawling, rate-limit bypassing, or automated abuse.
- Reverse engineering except where legally permitted.
- Uploading malware or harmful code.
- Violating third-party rights.
- Using the service to build competing datasets, benchmarks, or products if the business needs this restriction.

## 7. User Content

Use this section when users upload, submit, publish, store, generate, import, or share content.

Cover:

- Users retain ownership of their content unless otherwise stated.
- Users grant the service a license to host, process, transmit, display, and use content to provide the service.
- Users are responsible for having rights to their content.
- Moderation/removal rights when relevant.
- Backups, deletion, and export only if supported.

## 8. Generated Output Or AI Features

Use this section only when the product includes AI generation, automation, or model output.

Cover:

- Output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate.
- Users are responsible for reviewing output before relying on it.
- No professional advice unless explicitly offered and legally supported.
- Ownership/license treatment for prompts, inputs, and outputs if known.

## 9. Our Intellectual Property

State that the service, software, designs, trademarks, content, documentation, and related IP are owned by the company or licensors. Grant users a limited, revocable, non-transferable right to use the service according to the terms.

## 10. Feedback

State that users may provide feedback voluntarily and that the company may use it without obligation or compensation, unless the user requires a different policy.

## 11. Third-Party Services

Mention integrations, payment processors, hosting, analytics, identity providers, APIs, or external links when present.

State that third-party services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

## 12. Privacy

Reference the Privacy Policy if one exists. If not, use a placeholder and recommend creating one.

Do not duplicate a full privacy policy inside the terms.

## 13. Service Changes And Availability

State that the product may change, be updated, suspended, discontinued, or have features added or removed. Avoid SLA promises unless the project has a published SLA.

## 14. Suspension And Termination

Cover when the company may suspend or terminate access.

Common grounds:

- Terms violation.
- Payment failure.
- Security risk.
- Legal risk.
- Abuse or harm to other users, the service, or third parties.

Explain what happens after termination, including loss of access, outstanding payments, and survival of key sections.

## 15. Disclaimers

Use plain warranty-disclaimer language appropriate for the product.

Common structure:

The service is provided "as is" and "as available". We do not guarantee uninterrupted, secure, error-free, or complete operation, or that the service will meet every user need.

Avoid overbroad regulated claims if the product operates in finance, health, legal, education, employment, or other sensitive domains.

## 16. Limitation Of Liability

Limit liability for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages and lost profits, data, goodwill, or business interruption where legally permitted.

If the user provides a liability cap, include it. Otherwise use a placeholder such as `[Liability Cap]` and flag it for legal review.

## 17. Indemnification

Use when appropriate for user-generated content, business users, API users, marketplaces, or higher-risk services.

Common coverage:

- User content.
- User misuse.
- Violation of these terms.
- Violation of law or third-party rights.

## 18. Governing Law And Disputes

Include governing law and venue/arbitration only when provided by the user or existing docs.

If unknown, use placeholders and flag for legal review:

- `[Governing Law]`
- `[Courts/Venue]`
- `[Arbitration Terms, if any]`

## 19. Changes To These Terms

Explain how updates are made, when they take effect, and that continued use after changes means acceptance.

## 20. Contact

Include company contact details:

```text
[Company Legal Name]
[Mailing Address, if required]
[Support or Legal Email]
```

## Assumptions And Legal Review Notes

End with a short internal-facing section if facts are missing or if the user asked for a draft rather than publication-ready copy.

Include:

- Missing company/legal entity details.
- Missing jurisdiction or dispute terms.
- Missing refund/cancellation details.
- Unsupported compliance claims that were intentionally excluded.
- Clauses that require lawyer review before publication.

If creating a public-facing page, consider whether this section should stay in the Markdown source only or be removed from the rendered public copy.
